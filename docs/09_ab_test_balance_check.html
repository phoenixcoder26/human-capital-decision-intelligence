A balance check on 13 characteristics measured before anyone received the pilot.

How far apart were the groups before the pilot?

Each dot is the gap between the two group averages, in standard-deviation units. Dots inside the shaded band are close enough to treat as balanced.

How to read this

Standardized difference = (treatment average − control average) ÷ the pooled standard deviation. Under random assignment the gaps should be small, and a common rule of thumb treats anything within ±0.1 as balanced. With 13 comparisons, one or two dots landing outside the band can happen by chance, but each one should be named and considered when reading the results.

Simulated findings. These results come from a synthetic workforce and simulated outcomes; they are not real-world business outcomes. Source: R/03_ab_testing_retention_intervention.R.
