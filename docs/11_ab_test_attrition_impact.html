Did fewer employees leave?

Attrition in the simulated 3-month follow-up, with the size of the gap and how sure we can be about it.

38 of 266 treatment employees left (14.3%) against 39 of 267 control (14.6%). That 0.3-point gap is too small to tell apart from chance (p = 0.916).

Is that gap bigger than chance?

The line is the 95% confidence interval for the gap in attrition rates (control minus treatment). If it crosses the dashed zero line, chance can explain the gap.

0.3 percentage points (95% CI −5.65 to 6.29, p = 0.916). The adjusted odds ratio is 0.91 (95% CI 0.54 to 1.51), which also includes 1. A test this size reliably catches drops of about 7.5 points or more, so a smaller real effect could be hiding here.

Simulated findings. These results come from a synthetic workforce and simulated outcomes; they are not real-world business outcomes. Source: R/03_ab_testing_retention_intervention.R.
