Did the pilot raise engagement?

Engagement score change for every employee in the simulated experiment, treatment next to control.

Engagement rose about 4.6 points more in the treatment group than in control (95% CI 4.10 to 5.19, p < 0.001), a large difference.

Every employee's engagement change

Each dot is one employee. The curve shows where most employees landed. Zero means no change.

How to read this

Each curve is a smoothed picture of the dots beneath it. The vertical lines mark the group averages and the bracket at the top is the gap between them. Cohen's d measures that gap in standard deviations: 0.8 or more counts as large, and this one is 1.44.

Simulated findings. These results come from a synthetic workforce and simulated outcomes; they are not real-world business outcomes. Source: R/03_ab_testing_retention_intervention.R.
