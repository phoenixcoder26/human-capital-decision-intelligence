Who gained the most?

Engagement change in five employee groups, treatment versus control, with the interval for each gap.

Every group gained engagement under the pilot. The biggest gains were among high performers at risk and career-stalled employees.

Engagement gain by employee group

For each group, the gray dot is the control average and the teal dot is the treatment average. The bar behind the gap shows the 95% interval for that difference.

What to take from it

Every group gained engagement, and every interval stays above zero. The gap is widest for . Groups overlap (an employee can sit in more than one), and each holds only 32 to 65 employees per arm, so compare the pattern, not the exact ranking. Attrition within these groups is too sparse to read.

Simulated findings. These results come from a synthetic workforce and simulated outcomes; they are not real-world business outcomes. Source: R/03_ab_testing_retention_intervention.R.
